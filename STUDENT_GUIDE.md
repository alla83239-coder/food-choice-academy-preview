# Food Choice Academy — Student Guide

## Start
Enter your name once to open your learner profile. The mission map contains twelve missions.

## One screen at a time
Each objective question allows one response. A wrong answer reduces the score but does not block the next screen. After the response is recorded, press **Continue**.

## Progress
The mission Progress Bar shows your place in the active mission. The question bar shows progress within the current exercise.

## Reading and audio
Use **Next page** and **Back to page 1** to navigate a reading. Press **Play** to start audio. You may replay a recording for practice, but each test answer is recorded once.

## Smartphone assistants
- **Ms Harper** — project decisions, evidence, cost and sustainability.
- **Jess** — English, vocabulary and grammar.
- **Ben** — navigation and platform guidance.

The assistants provide hints, not answer keys.

## My Project
Open **My Project** from the side menu to review written work, task results, the menu, nutrition calculations, procurement, vending choices and the opening plan.

## Saving
Browser preview progress is stored in the current browser on the current device. Moodle uses SCORM 1.2 synchronisation. Do not clear browser data while using preview if you need to keep your work.

## Final project
Complete the required missions, open **Final Project**, review the proposal, complete the self-assessment and present the canteen business plan.
